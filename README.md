# Hi, I'm Trent

I'm a software engineer with over 25 years of experience in tech and IT. Most of my career has been spent figuring out creative technical solutions to modern problems. Sometimes that means writing software, and sometimes it means untangling a stubborn system or IT issue in a way that actually holds up over time.

Right now, I'm building [Fractal Fix Studios](https://fractalfixstudios.com), an independent studio where I work on apps, open-source tools, and eventually original video games.

### How I work

Anyone can use AI to spin up code in an afternoon now, often without much thought behind it. I use modern AI tools too, but I pair them with two and a half decades of hands-on engineering so what gets built is maintainable, practical, and made with intention.

More than anything, I'm trying to carry forward an older, simpler mindset about business: provide real value at a fair cost, treat people right, and earn an honest living for my family and anyone I work with. I believe business done honestly can still succeed and thrive without dark patterns, farming user data, or locking people in. If you use something I build, you should know what it costs, know your data isn't being sold, and feel free to walk away or come back whenever you want.
